# Known Issues — Fulfillment

Known Issues for the Fulfillment module are sourced from the VTEX Known Issues repository, maintained by the Product Support team:

**→ [vtexdocs/known-issues](https://github.com/vtexdocs/known-issues)**

That repository is the source of truth for all Known Issues published on VTEX Help Center. Issues are created by the Product Support team and automatically synced there. Do not create Known Issues directly in this folder.

## How to find Fulfillment Known Issues

In the [vtexdocs/known-issues](https://github.com/vtexdocs/known-issues) repo, Known Issues are organized under `docs/en/known-issues/` by product team. Look for the `Fulfillment` subfolder.

## Subfolders in this directory

The subfolders here (`helpful`, `important`, `no-fix`, `not-classified`) reflect the classification used internally to track and prioritize Known Issues by their resolution status and impact level.
