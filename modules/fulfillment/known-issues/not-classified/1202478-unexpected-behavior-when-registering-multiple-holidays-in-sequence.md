# KI 1202478 — Unexpected Behavior When Registering Multiple Holidays in Sequence

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Area** | Logistics |
| **Status** | Backlog |

**Description:** When registering multiple holidays in sequence, unexpected behaviors can occur. Some previously registered holidays may be deleted, and some new holidays may not be registered correctly.

**Workaround:** Space out the creation of holidays by defining an interval of at least 1 minute between each registration.
