# KI 1207733 — Carriers shipping method agendada

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Area** | Logistics / Shipping |
| **Status** | Backlog |

**Description:** The "agendada" (scheduled) shipping method for certain carriers does not behave correctly in the logistics configuration. Scheduled delivery windows may not be displayed or available to shoppers at checkout even when properly configured in the carrier shipping policy.

**Workaround:** Review the carrier's shipping policy configuration and ensure the scheduled delivery windows are correctly set up. If the issue persists, contact VTEX support.
