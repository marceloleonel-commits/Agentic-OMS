# KI 379685 — Shipping Policy Not Prioritized Due 'Reason: Dismissed as a Matter of Priority' Even with Better Cost

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Area** | Logistics |
| **Status** | Backlog |

**Description:** When a cost is set from the warehouse to the dock in the shipping rate, it can affect the selection of the shipping policy. Since dock cost is not included in the prioritization of shipping policies, a more expensive shipping policy can be selected over a cheaper one. For example: Policy X at $7.00 shipping + $0.00 dock = $7.00 total vs Policy Y at $6.00 shipping + $2.00 dock = $8.00 total. Y is selected because the dock cost is excluded from prioritization logic.

**Workaround:** No workaround available for this issue.
