# KI 380471 — Shipping Policy with Incorrect MaxVolume on Spreadsheet Does Not Show Message Error on Simulation

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Area** | Logistics |
| **Status** | No Fix |

**Description:** When the Shipping Policy has a MaxVolume value in the spreadsheet that is lower than the product's volume calculation, the shipping policy is correctly discarded during simulation. However, no error message is displayed to indicate why the policy was not applied, leaving the merchant unaware of the reason.

**Workaround:** There is no workaround for this case.
