# Sort Search Results: Available Products First

## Problem Statement

When a shopper's location is known, the system knows which products can be delivered or picked up at their address and which cannot. Yet today, unavailable products appear interspersed with available ones throughout search results. Shoppers waste attention on products they cannot buy, and merchants waste premium listing positions on inventory that will fail at checkout.

## Vision

When a shopper's location is set, products available for delivery or pickup at that location appear first in search results. Unavailable or out-of-stock products are sorted last — visible for catalog completeness when the merchant chooses, but never crowding out buyable inventory.

Merchants control whether unavailable products are shown at all (via the "Show Out-of-Stock Product" Catalog setting). When shown, the system guarantees they never outrank available products in the listing.

## Target Users

**Merchants:** Omnichannel retailers using Intelligent Search and Delivery Promise where availability varies significantly by shopper location. Particularly valuable for merchants with large catalogs and location-restricted inventory.

**Shoppers:** Anyone browsing with a known location who wants results that reflect what they can actually buy — without scrolling past unavailable items to find purchasable products.

## Success Metrics

- Reduction in time-to-first-available-product in search results for location-aware sessions
- Conversion uplift from improved result relevance in location-aware sessions
- % of location-aware sessions where no unavailable products appear on the first results page

## Out of Scope

- Sorting by delivery speed within available products (handled by Search Filters)
- Personalized sorting based on purchase history
- Relevance signals beyond availability (price, reviews, click-through rate)
