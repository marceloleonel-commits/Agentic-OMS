## MMR

**Title:** Delivery Promise — Filter by Specific Pickup Point in PLP

**Description:** With this release, shoppers will be able to filter PLP and search results to show only products available at a specific pickup point they choose. This means shoppers who want to collect from a particular store see only the products actually stocked and available there — eliminating the frustration of selecting a pickup store at checkout only to find items unavailable.

**Availability:** Closed Beta · H1 2025

**Target Audience:**
- Tier: All tiers
- Persona: Shopper / E-commerce Manager
- Pain: When a shopper wants to pick up from a specific store (e.g., "Kopenhagen Morumbi"), they have no way to pre-filter the assortment to that store before reaching checkout. They add items to cart, then discover some are not available at their chosen store.
- Use Case: Let shoppers select a specific pickup point and filter all PLP results to show only what can be collected there

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Filter to select a specific pickup point from the list of available stores/lockers within the shopper's radius
- Assortment filters to products available at the selected pickup point
- Filter applies to the current PLP only (not persistent across pages — sitewide persistence is a separate MMR)

**Not in scope:** Pickup nearby radius filter (separate MMR), sitewide persistent store filter (separate MMR), pickup point ordering by distance (handled by Pickup Point Listing MMR).
