## MMR

**Title:** Delivery Promise — Filter by Pickup Nearby in PLP

**Description:** With this release, shoppers will be able to filter PLP and search results to show only products available for pickup within a configured radius of their location — without having to select a specific store. This means shoppers who want to pick up but haven't chosen a store yet can see what's available nearby, narrowing the assortment to products that can actually be collected before they enter checkout.

**Availability:** Closed Beta · H1 2025

**Target Audience:**
- Tier: All tiers
- Persona: Shopper / E-commerce Manager
- Pain: Shoppers who want to pick up an item can't see at a glance which products have any pickup option near them. Browsing the full assortment means encountering products with no local pickup availability.
- Use Case: Filter PLP to show only products available for pickup within the shopper's configured radius, without requiring them to choose a specific store first

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- "Pickup Nearby" filter option on PLP that shows products available at any pickup point within the configured radius (default 50 km)
- Filter uses the same radius logic as Checkout and the Shipping Method filter
- Filter applies to the current PLP only (not persistent across pages)
- Shopper can update their ZIP directly within the filter

**Not in scope:** Selection of a specific pickup point (separate MMR), sitewide persistent store filter (separate MMR), radius configuration by the shopper (radius is Logistics-configured).
