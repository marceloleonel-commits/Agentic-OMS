## MMR

**Title:** Delivery Promise — Filter by Delivery Option in PLP

**Description:** With this release, shoppers will be able to filter PLP and search results by a specific delivery option — such as Express, Same-Day, or Scheduled Delivery — showing only products available through that option. This means shoppers who need a specific delivery type (e.g., same-day delivery for a gift) can narrow the assortment before browsing, instead of discovering the mismatch at checkout.

**Availability:** Open Beta · H2 2025

**Target Audience:**
- Tier: All tiers using Intelligent Search and Delivery Promise with Fulfillment Catalog configured
- Persona: Shopper / E-commerce Manager
- Pain: Shoppers with specific delivery requirements (e.g., same-day, express, scheduled) cannot pre-filter PLP results to those options. They discover delivery type unavailability only at checkout.
- Use Case: Filter PLP to show only products available through the shopper's required delivery option for their ZIP

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Delivery option filter on PLP (e.g., Express, Same-Day, Scheduled Delivery) driven by the merchant's Fulfillment Catalog configuration
- Filters to products with the selected delivery option available for the shopper's ZIP
- Filter applies to the current PLP only
- Available delivery options are surfaced dynamically based on what is configured by the merchant

**Not in scope:** Dynamic delivery estimate filter (separate MMR), shipping method filter (separate MMR), sitewide persistence (separate MMR).
