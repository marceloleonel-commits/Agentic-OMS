## MMR

**Title:** Delivery Promise — Delivery Availability Badge on Product Cards

**Description:** With this release, product cards on PLPs and search results will display a badge indicating whether a product is available for delivery or pickup to the shopper's location. This means shoppers can immediately identify products that can actually reach them — without clicking into the PDP and without reaching checkout to discover unavailability.

**Availability:** Closed Beta · H1 2025

**Target Audience:**
- Tier: All tiers using Intelligent Search and Delivery Promise
- Persona: Shopper / E-commerce Manager
- Pain: Shoppers cannot tell from a product card whether an item is deliverable to their location. They click into PDPs and add items to carts only to find at checkout that the product cannot be delivered to their ZIP.
- Use Case: Surface a clear availability signal on product cards in PLPs so shoppers can make informed browsing decisions before clicking through

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Availability badge on product cards indicating delivery availability for the shopper's ZIP (e.g., "Available for delivery", "Available for pickup")
- Badge reflects Delivery Promise availability — consistent with Checkout availability for the same ZIP
- Shown on PLP product cards and search result cards
- Badge is not shown when no location is set (fallback assortment)

**Not in scope:** Dynamic delivery estimate on cards (separate MMR), specific delivery option label on cards (separate MMR), availability badge on PDP (handled separately).
