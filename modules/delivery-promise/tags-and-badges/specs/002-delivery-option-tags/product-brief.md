## MMR

**Title:** Delivery Promise — Delivery Option Tags on Product Cards

**Description:** With this release, product cards on PLPs and search results will display tags indicating which delivery options are available for that product at the shopper's location — for example, "Express Delivery" or "Same-Day Delivery." This means shoppers can see at a glance which products offer the delivery options they need, without navigating to the PDP or reaching checkout.

**Availability:** Open Beta · H2 2025

**Target Audience:**
- Tier: All tiers using Intelligent Search, Delivery Promise, and Fulfillment Catalog
- Persona: Shopper / E-commerce Manager
- Pain: Shoppers cannot tell from a product card which delivery options (express, same-day, scheduled) are available for a given item. They click through to PDPs or reach checkout to discover the options are not available.
- Use Case: Surface delivery option labels (e.g., "Express Delivery", "Same-Day") on product cards to help shoppers identify relevant products at a glance

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Delivery option tags on product cards, driven by the merchant's Fulfillment Catalog configuration and Delivery Promise
- Tags are shown for options available at the shopper's ZIP (e.g., "Express Delivery", "Same-Day Delivery")
- Merchant-configurable tag labels and which options to display
- Shown on PLP product cards and search result cards

**Not in scope:** Dynamic delivery estimate tags (separate MMR — shows a specific date/time), delivery availability badge (separate MMR — binary available/unavailable), delivery option tags on PDP (handled separately).
