## MMR

**Title:** Delivery Promise — Dynamic Delivery Estimate Tags on Product Cards

**Description:** With this release, product cards on PLPs and search results will display dynamic, real-time delivery estimates — such as "Arrives today", "Arrives tomorrow", or "Arrives by Friday" — computed for the shopper's ZIP at the moment of browsing. This means shoppers can see exactly when an item will arrive before clicking through, reducing cart abandonment caused by late delivery discovery.

**Availability:** Open Beta · H2 2025

**Target Audience:**
- Tier: All tiers using Intelligent Search and Delivery Promise
- Persona: Shopper / E-commerce Manager
- Pain: Shoppers cannot see delivery timelines from the PLP. They must navigate to PDPs or reach checkout to discover when an item will arrive. This friction leads to cart abandonment when the estimated date doesn't meet their needs.
- Use Case: Display a real-time, personalized delivery estimate on each product card so shoppers can make faster, more confident purchase decisions

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Dynamic delivery estimate tag on product cards (e.g., "Arrives today", "Arrives tomorrow", "Arrives by [date]") computed by Delivery Promise for the shopper's ZIP in real time
- Estimate reflects the fastest available delivery option for the shopper's ZIP
- Shown on PLP product cards and search result cards
- Merchant-configurable label format (e.g., "Get it by [date]" vs. "Arrives [date]")

**Not in scope:** Delivery option tags (separate MMR — shows option names, not estimated dates), availability badge (separate MMR — binary signal), estimate tags on PDP (handled separately).
