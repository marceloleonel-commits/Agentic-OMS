## MMR

**Title:** Delivery Promise — Delivery-Aware Campaign Landing Pages

**Description:** With this release, merchants will be able to create campaign landing pages (e.g., "Get it today", "Same-day delivery on gifts") that are dynamically populated with products available for a specific delivery promise at the shopper's location. This means campaign pages reflect real, fulfillable inventory — not a static curated list — so shoppers landing on a "same-day gifts" page always see products they can actually receive in time.

**Availability:** Open Beta · H2 2025

**Target Audience:**
- Tier: All tiers using Intelligent Search and Delivery Promise
- Persona: E-commerce Manager / Merchandiser
- Pain: Merchants create "Get it today" or "Same-day delivery" campaign pages with manually curated product lists that quickly become stale. Products that no longer qualify for the promised delivery remain on the page, leading to cart abandonment when shoppers reach checkout.
- Use Case: Allow merchants to create campaign pages where the product grid is dynamically filtered by Delivery Promise availability, so the page always reflects fulfillable products for the shopper's ZIP and selected delivery criteria

---

## Scope

**In scope:**
- Campaign landing pages where the product grid is powered by Delivery Promise availability for the shopper's ZIP
- Merchants configure the delivery criteria (e.g., "same-day only", "arrives within 24h") that define the page's assortment
- Product grid updates dynamically as the shopper's location changes
- Available for FastStore and VTEX IO storefronts

**Not in scope:** Static curated campaign pages (existing functionality), banners with delivery messaging on PLPs (separate MMR), sort ordering by delivery estimate (Sort & Search Results MMR).
