# Banners & Discovery Pages

## Problem Statement

Merchants run campaigns around fast fulfillment — "Get it today," "Same-day delivery," "Ready for pickup in 1 hour" — but today they cannot build landing pages or banners that dynamically show only products that actually fulfill that promise for the shopper's location. They either hardcode a curated product list (which becomes stale as stock and logistics change) or show a static banner with no product tie-in. Neither option delivers the full value of the campaign.

## Vision

Merchants can create delivery-promise-aware discovery surfaces: banners and landing pages that are dynamically populated with products available under a specific delivery promise for the shopper's location. A "Get it today" campaign page shows only products the shopper can actually receive same-day. A "Pickup available near you" banner surfaces only products with a pickup point within the shopper's radius — updated in real time as availability changes.

These surfaces are powered by Delivery Promise + Intelligent Search: the merchant defines the delivery condition (same-day delivery, next-day, specific pickup store), and the system resolves which products meet that condition for each shopper's location at the time of the session.

## Target Users

**Merchants:** Marketing and e-commerce teams who run delivery-based campaigns and want campaign performance to reflect actual fulfillment capability, not manually maintained product lists.

**Shoppers:** Shoppers with time-sensitive needs who want to discover products they can receive quickly without searching and filtering manually.

## Success Metrics

- Campaign conversion rate on delivery-promise-aware landing pages vs. static curated pages
- % of merchants using Delivery Promise that create at least one delivery-aware campaign
- Accuracy: 0 products shown in a campaign that do not meet the campaign's delivery promise for the shopper's location

## Out of Scope

- Delivery fee promotions (free shipping campaigns are a separate feature)
- Personalized delivery campaigns based on shopper purchase history (future)
- Integration with external campaign management tools (future)
