---

## Problem

VTEX storefronts show products that cannot be delivered to the shopper's location. 24% of Tier 1 carts contain at least one unavailable item; 28% of those fail because there is no valid delivery route (`cannotBeDelivered`). Merchants cannot show delivery dates, SLA filters, or method badges during navigation — a proven conversion lever. Delivery Promise is live with 6 merchants and has a waiting list of 100+ merchants, but activation requires bespoke VTEX professional services engagement that cannot scale.

---

## Solution

Expand Delivery Promise to Open Beta with a self-serve onboarding path. Enable native SLA filters and badges on PLP and PDP across Store Framework, FastStore, and headless storefronts — without custom development. Publish agency implementation guidelines so partners can activate merchants independently. Improve service resilience and cover outstanding uncovered scenarios.

---

## Who Benefits

**Ecommerce Operations Managers at Tier 1 omnichannel merchants** can activate Delivery Promise independently via the self-serve Onboarding Service, surface accurate delivery dates and SLA options from the first page load, and enable delivery method filters and badges without requiring VTEX professional services.

**Shoppers** see only products that can actually be delivered to their location, with delivery dates and method options visible from the product listing page — reducing cart abandonment caused by discovering unavailability at checkout.

---

## Definition of Done

- [ ] 12+ merchants activated via self-serve Onboarding Service — measured by onboarding completion events, not manual VTEX team sign-offs
- [ ] Cart Availability for newly activated Open Beta merchants reaches ≥93.2% within 4 weeks of activation
- [ ] SLA filters and badges available natively on PLP and PDP for Store Framework and FastStore without custom development
- [ ] ≥80% of Open Beta activations completed by agencies without VTEX professional services intervention
- [ ] Zero Checkout throttling incidents caused by Delivery Promise indexing for any Open Beta merchant
- [ ] Agency implementation guidelines published
